# Change Control Policy (Synthetic Demo)

This synthetic document exists only to demonstrate EvidenceTrace. It is not a real
policy and must not be used for operational decisions.

## Change classification

A change is high risk when it affects customer data, changes an authorization
boundary, or modifies a production safety control. High-risk changes require a
written risk assessment and a rollback plan before implementation.

## Review and approval

A high-risk change must be reviewed by an independent reviewer and approved by the
service owner. Emergency changes may proceed only when an incident commander records
the reason, the scope, and the follow-up review deadline. Requests outside this
policy are escalated to the governance committee.

## Evidence retention

The change record must include the implementation date, source version, reviewer,
approval decision, and test evidence. Retain the record for one year.
